# DECISION RULES

## Rule 1 — One next skill

At any moment, there is one primary technical skill.

Do not study five new technologies simultaneously.

## Rule 2 — Gates before progression

Move forward after:
- understanding
- practice
- project
- debugging
- explanation

Documentation is allowed. Blind copying is not.

## Rule 3 — Build before collecting resources

One course + docs + project beats ten courses.

## Rule 4 — Technology requires a reason

Add a new language/tool only if:
- university requires it,
- a target job repeatedly requires it,
- the current architecture genuinely benefits from it, or
- it fills a clear systems gap.

## Rule 5 — Do not chase titles

A Backend Engineer role can be better than a badly designed "DevOps Engineer" role if it provides strong systems, cloud and production experience.

## Rule 6 — Optimize for career capital

When comparing two opportunities, evaluate:
1. technical learning
2. professional credibility
3. production exposure
4. future Japan relevance
5. compensation
6. timeline impact

## Rule 7 — No certificate-first learning

Priority:
skill > proof/project > experience > communication > certification.

## Rule 8 — No giant roadmap rewrites

Review monthly. Change the roadmap only when assumptions materially change.

## Rule 9 — Japan is not the only reason to learn

Build portable skills so Europe, Japan, remote work and other countries remain possible.

## Rule 10 — Do not confuse exposure with mastery

"I watched it" is not "I can do it."

## Rule 11 — Production beats toy complexity

One system you deploy, monitor, break and repair is more valuable than many tutorial clones.

## Rule 12 — AI comes after systems

Do not replace foundational engineering with an early AI obsession.

## Rule 13 — Freelancing is optional

Only sell work you can reliably deliver.

## Rule 14 — Protect the age window

Your Japan/PR goal is age-constrained. Avoid needless detours that add years without adding proportionate career value.

## Rule 15 — Immigration numbers must be verified

Treat third-party HSP/PR calculators as planning tools. Verify current official Japanese rules before making legal/financial decisions.
