# EUROPE → JAPAN TRANSITION

## Goal

Move to Japan as an experienced professional, not as a beginner.

## Timing

Start serious Japan-employer research during the second European professional year.

Do not wait until the third year ends to learn the market.

Ideal transition window: roughly age 25–27, depending on graduation age, experience and job market.

## Japanese job profile

Target:
- Cloud Engineer
- DevOps Engineer
- SRE
- Platform Engineer
- Infrastructure Engineer
- Software Engineer with cloud/platform ownership

## Employer strategy

Tier A:
- multinational/product companies
- international engineering teams
- companies demonstrably hiring foreign engineers

Tier B:
- Japanese tech companies with English-friendly engineering teams

Tier C:
- traditional Japanese companies where stronger Japanese capability is required

Do not build the entire plan around a few famous company names.

## Candidate profile at transition

Ideal:
- relevant Bachelor's degree
- 3+ years professional European experience
- production Cloud/DevOps/SRE work
- Linux and networking depth
- Docker
- Terraform
- CI/CD
- observability/security
- Kubernetes
- Japanese N2 or stronger, ideally N1/business ability
- strong professional English

## Application preparation

Maintain:
- English CV
- Japanese CV when appropriate
- project portfolio
- system architecture examples
- incident stories
- measurable achievements
- references
- degree/transcript documents
- experience certificates
- salary evidence
- Japanese certification records

## Japan move rule

Do not move merely because Japan is emotionally attractive.

Move when the Japanese job materially improves the overall strategy:
- relevant role
- lawful status
- adequate compensation
- credible career progression
- manageable living situation
- HSP/PR viability

## Career after moving

Do not mentally restart as a junior.

Your European experience is supposed to be the asset you export into Japan.

## First Japan years

Priority order:
1. employment stability
2. legal compliance
3. workplace Japanese
4. technical performance
5. salary progression
6. HSP/PR documentation
7. long-term settlement planning
