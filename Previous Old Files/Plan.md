# 🕹️ 1. What is HSP?

**HSP = Highly Skilled Professional.**

Japanese immigration has a points system for certain highly skilled foreign professionals.

Imagine Japan gives your future professional profile a character sheet:

|Your real-life stat|HSP equivalent|
|---|---|
|How young are you?|Age|
|How educated are you?|Academic background|
|How experienced are you?|Work experience|
|How much do you earn?|Annual salary|
|How good is your Japanese?|Japanese proficiency|
|Special achievements|Research|
|Relevant licences|Japanese national qualifications|
|Special employer/job circumstances|Organization bonuses|

Japan then adds those points.

The normal threshold for HSP recognition is **70 points**. The system has three broad HSP activity categories; for you, the relevant one is generally **Advanced Specialized/Technical Activities**, because you're planning an IT/Cloud/DevOps career.

---

# 🏆 2. Why do you care about 70 and 80?

Because of the special PR route.

Under the HSP accelerated PR framework:

### 70+ points

The ordinary residence period can be reduced to **3 years** under the applicable route.

### 80+ points

It can be reduced to **1 year** under the applicable route.

But there is an important detail people often skip:

The government doesn't merely say:

> "I have 80 today, give me PR."

The official guidance says the 80-point route requires you to have been continuously in Japan for at least one year while meeting the applicable highly-skilled criteria, **or** to be deemed to have had 80+ points when calculated with reference to the situation one year before the PR application. The 70-point route similarly has the three-year/reference-period requirements.

And of course:

**80 points ≠ automatic PR.**

It gives you the accelerated eligibility framework. The PR application still has other requirements.
