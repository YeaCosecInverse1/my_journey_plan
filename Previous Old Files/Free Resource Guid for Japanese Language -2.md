One important correction first: JLPT does not simply give you a normal percentage score. Its results use scaled scores. 4. Step 3 — Pronunciation

Do this before becoming heavily dependent on English pronunciation guides.

Japanese pronunciation is relatively systematic, but you still need to learn:

- mora timing
- long vowels
- small っ
- ん
- contracted sounds
- devoicing
- rhythm
- pitch accent
- sentence intonation

Do not obsess over pitch accent on Day 1.

However:

«Learn good pronunciation from the beginning instead of intentionally developing an English accent and trying to remove it years later.»

Resource
