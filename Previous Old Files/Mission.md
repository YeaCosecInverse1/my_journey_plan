---

# Core Philosophy

Most people choose one:

- Learn
    
- Earn
    

This roadmap chooses both.

Every phase must satisfy:

1. Increase technical skill.
    
2. Increase market value.
    
3. Increase future leverage.
    

If a skill does not help at least one of those, it is optional.

---

# The North Star

The destination is NOT:

- Frontend Developer
    
- Backend Developer
    
- DevOps Engineer
    
- AI Engineer
    

The destination is:

## AI Systems Integration Engineer

Someone capable of:

- Understanding business problems
