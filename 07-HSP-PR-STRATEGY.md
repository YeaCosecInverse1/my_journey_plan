# JAPAN HSP + PR STRATEGY

## Core objective

Use the Highly Skilled Professional points system as a planning framework, with 80+ points as the preferred target when realistically achievable.

## Main score-building levers

- age
- academic background
- qualifying university bonus where applicable
- professional experience
- annual income
- Japanese language ability
- other currently eligible bonus categories

Always verify the current official tables and exact qualification rules before relying on a point calculation.

## Why 80+ is preferred

The current PR fast-track framework generally distinguishes:
- 70+ points: potentially the 3-year route
- 80+ points: potentially the 1-year route

These are not automatic PR guarantees. Actual residence, conduct, public obligations, documents and other legal requirements still matter.

## Strategy

### Education

Obtain a strong relevant degree and, when economically rational, prefer a university that qualifies for the relevant HSP university bonus.

### Age

Use the young-age advantage while it exists. Do not delay the Japan transition unnecessarily just to accumulate points that are less valuable than the age advantage you lose.

### Experience

Accumulate at least 3 years of strong professional European experience, then reassess the best Japan entry timing.

### Salary

Move toward higher-value engineering work. Do not chase salary alone at the expense of technical trajectory, but recognize that compensation can strongly affect HSP points.

### Japanese

Target N2 as a serious professional threshold and N1/business Japanese as the stronger end state.

## HSP calculation discipline

Maintain a personal score sheet with:
- current points
- evidence for every claimed category
- missing evidence
- conservative score
- optimistic score
- expiry/validity concerns

Never plan using only the optimistic number.

## Evidence archive

Keep:
- degree
- transcript
- university qualification evidence where relevant
- employment contracts
- experience certificates
- payslips
- tax records
- insurance/social obligation records
- Japanese certificates
- professional certifications
- awards/publications if applicable
- identity/residence records

## Important source hierarchy

1. Japanese Immigration Services Agency / Ministry of Justice: legal authority.
2. Official university/company documents.
3. Third-party calculators such as JapanPR: planning tools.

Do not treat third-party calculators as government decisions.

## PR timing concept

Ideal theoretical sequence:

Move to Japan around age 25–27 → maintain the qualifying high-skill profile → use the applicable HSP PR fast track once all actual requirements are satisfied.

The plan must be revalidated against the rules in force at the time of application.
